# CommandC 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-11-20

## 简体中文

CommandC 是一款 iPhone 局域网素材传输工具。你可以主动将照片、截图和短视频发送到自己选择的已配对电脑。CommandC 无需注册账号，开发者不会通过本应用收集或接收你的素材、设备数据或使用记录。应用没有广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

- **素材与权限**。相机用于拍摄和扫描配对码。麦克风用于你主动开始的相机录像。系统照片选择器或分享菜单只向应用提供你所选的素材。跨 App 屏幕采集需要你从应用发起并在 iOS 系统界面授权，再由你主动截取、录制或停止。你可以在 iPhone「设置」中管理已授予的权限。
- **发送与配对**。开启本地网络权限后，CommandC 在本地网络查找已配对电脑，并通过加密连接把选定素材发送给你确认的接收端。素材不会经由开发者的服务器中转。配对凭据存放在 iOS 钥匙串。你可以在「连接电脑」里移除配对，也可以在电脑端撤销手机的权限。
- **保留与删除**。待发素材的副本暂存在应用的私有目录。成功发送或主动放弃后，暂存副本会被清理。发送失败时暂存副本用于重试，应用也会清理遗留的临时文件。相册原件由你在「照片」中管理。接收端的缓存、系统剪贴板以及你粘贴或另存的文件由你在电脑上管理。

有关隐私或数据处理的问题，可以通过项目的 GitHub 支持渠道联系我们。GitHub 是独立服务，公开问题会被其他人看到。[CommandC 支持](https://github.com/Flechazo-YQ/suishouchuan/issues)

## English

CommandC is an iPhone utility for transferring media over a local network. You choose which photos, screenshots, or short videos to send and which paired computer receives them. The app requires no account. The developer does not collect or receive your media, device data, or usage information through the app. It includes no ads, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

- **Media and permissions.** Camera access is used to take photos and scan pairing codes. Microphone access supports camera video that you start. The system photo picker and share sheet provide only the items you select. Cross-app screen capture starts at your request, requires iOS authorization, and lets you choose when to take a screenshot, record, or stop. You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings.
- **Transfer and pairing.** Local network permission lets CommandC find paired computers and send selected media over an encrypted connection to your chosen receiver. The developer does not relay your files through a server. Pairing credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. You can remove a computer in CommandC or revoke the phone from the desktop receiver.
- **Retention and deletion.** Pending copies are kept in the app’s private storage. The app clears temporary copies after a successful transfer or when you discard them. It retains failed transfers for retry and clears old temporary files. You manage originals in Photos and control the receiver’s cache, clipboard contents, and any saved or pasted copies on your own computer.

For privacy or data-handling questions, contact the project through GitHub. GitHub is a separate service and issues are public. [CommandC support](https://github.com/Flechazo-YQ/suishouchuan/issues)
